# The cord: direction

Date: 2026-11-08. Surface mode: Experience. Confidence: reference-light (no Mobbin tools in this session, so the references below are historical objects, not screens). Approval gate waived by the owner for this exploration.

Subject: one PayPal hold is an agent's budget. The world it touches is a ledger of others' money kept by binding. The oldest knotted-cord ledger is the Andean quipu, so the product is drawn as one.

Idea: the lease is a cord. Its length is the $20.00 hold. Pendants hang where each paid call was paid, and the amount is tied as knots by decimal place (tens, dollars, dimes, cents).

Cord colors (the only data encoding):
- captured: walnut dye, solid knots
- owed, not captured: bare cotton with hatching, outlined knots
- free to spend: cochineal, the single accent

Palette, each named for a material: quarry limestone ground #D5D8CE, loom lint surface #E4E6DC, walnut ink #231F1B, llama umber muted ink #54504A, undyed cotton #F3EFE2, cochineal #8C1D38. Not the cream, serif, clay cluster: the ground is a cool grey-green and the accent is a deep carmine used on one meaning.

Type: Familjen Grotesk for display and UI, Spline Sans Mono only for ids, times and prices in tables. Rejected: Instrument Serif (the default pick of generated pages), Gloock (fashion serif, drifts toward the known cream cluster).

Signature element: the cord itself, with a vertical version on phones (the same drawing transposed).

Authored motion moment: pendants settle onto the cord on first paint of Lease (under 450 ms). New calls drop on. Void cuts the free length away. Everything else answers a click.

Evidence gate: Approve, Settle capture and Void use plain buttons and typed confirmation. The cord is feedback and reading, never the control on a money path.

Truth labels: every figure is `measured` (solid underline), `computed` (dashed underline) or `fixture` (italic). Proof is the only view with measured data and carries no fixture.

Chart themes used as constraints, never as ornament: held water becomes held length, binding as care becomes the cord, exact ledger craft becomes knots that are countable.

Open: not tested with a person, not tested on a physical phone, dark theme not built.
